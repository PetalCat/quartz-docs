# [Extended Learning Opportunities](https://phs.psdr3.org/academics/extended-learning-opportunities)
## Description
An Extended Learning Opportunity (ELO) allows students to earn credit through independent projects, internships, or community-based learning experiences beyond the traditional classroom.

## Program Highlights
 - Flexibility: Highly individualized learning paths tailored to student interests 
 - Community Partnerships: Developed with school and community members to meet specific competencies  
 - Engagement: Minimum of 60 hours of hands-on learning  
 - Culmination: Includes a project and presentation to demonstrate competency  
 - Credit Awarded: Upon fulfillment of all program requirements
